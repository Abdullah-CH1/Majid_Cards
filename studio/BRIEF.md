# Creative brief - Wedding Invite Studio samples

Client: a Pakistani wedding-invitation designer (Lahore). They send this single HTML file to couples so the couple can
browse a gallery of **40+ complete sample designs**, replay each one, and pick a favourite; the designer then renders
the chosen one to an MP4 with `html2mp4.py`. Every sample must feel like a premium, distinct product - the kind of
animated e-invite people pay for on Etsy / Instagram - and work for both the **Baraat** (richer, deeper colours:
maroon, crimson, emerald, antique gold) and the **Walima** (lighter, softer: ivory, champagne, blush, sage, powder
blue, silver, rose gold).

The couple's own idea that must shine: **pop-up story moments** - the dulha's (groom's) name comes in, then a dulha
illustration (flat "icon" style), then the dulhan (bride) illustration and the dulhan's name - plus lots of other
ideas.

Tone: joyful, elegant, respectful (Muslim wedding: Bismillah, Mughal architecture, lanterns, roses & jasmine,
crescent & stars, mehndi, doli, ghodi, fairy lights, truck art...). Never cheap, never cluttered, never cartoonish
except where playful is the point (truck art, gift box) - even then, crafted.

Research (read what is useful to you):
* `studio/research/concepts.json` - storyboards and build notes for many concepts
* `studio/research/motion.json` - motion-design techniques with CSS/SVG snippets and pacing rules
* `studio/research/culture.json` - events, phrases, illustration notes, dos and don'ts
* `studio/research/palettes.json` - contrast-checked Baraat / Walima palettes
* `studio/research/typography.json` - Google Fonts that are verified to exist, with pairings

The original three openings (envelope, palace doors, golden medallion) in `studio/core/core.css` +
`studio/template.html` show the house style: SVG drawn with currentColor/palette variables, `cqw` sizing, staggered
eased keyframes, gold hairlines, Great Vibes + Cormorant Garamond. Match or exceed that polish.
