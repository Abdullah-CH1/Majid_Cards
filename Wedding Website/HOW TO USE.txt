WEDDING WEBSITE - Abdul Aleem & Laiba (Baraat)
================================================

OPEN IT
  Double-click  index.html  (Chrome / Edge / phone browser). Internet on for the fonts.
  Best viewed on a phone, or on a computer it shows as a phone-width page in the middle.

WHAT HAPPENS (same flow as the reference video)
  1. Sculpted mauve envelope with ginkgo leaves and a wax seal (A L monogram). Tap the seal.
  2. The flap opens, the envelope falls away: pearly satin tied with a sheer organza bow.
  3. The bow unties and the ribbon slides off both sides, then the satin parts from the centre.
  4. The embossed rose card - the wording fades in line by line.
  5. Scroll: live countdown ticket, "See you soon!"
  6. Mother-of-pearl section with a pearl frame - guests scratch the oval to reveal your photo.
  7. Location: venue, two short paragraphs, venue sketch, GET DIRECTIONS (opens Google Maps).
  Music button bottom-right (appears once you add a song).

CHANGE THE WORDING
  Open  config.js  in Notepad, change the text between the quotes, save, refresh the page.
  (names, date, Baraat time, venue, countdown date/time, location text, Google Maps search, footer)

ADD YOUR PHOTO (scratch-to-reveal)
  Replace  assets\photo.jpg  with the couple's photo, same file name. Portrait photos fit best.

ADD MUSIC
  Put a song in the assets folder named  music.mp3 . The music button then shows up by itself;
  the song starts when the guest taps the seal.

PUT IT ONLINE (so you can send a link on WhatsApp)
  Upload the whole folder (index.html, style.css, app.js, config.js, assets) to any static host,
  e.g. Netlify Drop (drag the folder onto app.netlify.com/drop) or GitHub Pages.

FOR LATER (technical)
  tools\make_assets.py  re-generates every texture (envelope, seal, satin, bow, card, pearl, sketch).
  The seal monogram letters are set at the top of the "wax seal" part of that file (CONFIG_MONO).
